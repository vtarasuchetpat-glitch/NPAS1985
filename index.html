About NPAS

Nanbargal Podhu Arivu Sangam (NPAS) is a non-commercial social welfare association established on 2 April 1985 at Chetpet, Chennai.

Tamil Nadu Society Registration No. 67/1985.

NPAS promotes social service, education, health awareness, fellowship and community welfare.
